## D-S6 scope and trade-off

An owner-requested draft may be written by a model that has **several chats in context**. This removes the previous isolated owner-writer handoff and avoids losing the owner's instructions between models; it increases the risk of unintended cross-chat material entering a proposal. This is the owner's explicit “one brain” choice, not the review recommendation.

The approval card must show **recipient and full text before any send**, label generated text as **written by the assistant**, and bind approval to the current version. The strict propose tool creates an action; it cannot send. Approval and the immediate policy re-check remain the gate. Consent and account scoping apply to every included item. Exact dictation is owner-authored only when the application copies verified source characters; AI rewrites receive generated authorship per version.

**Contact-triggered runs keep AGENTS §6.3a/4 isolation unchanged:** no tools, structured outputs, one conversation/contact's permitted context. Future DELEG-DRAFT stays separately scoped and is not converted into a multi-chat owner_request path. This record does not edit any §6 invariant text; H4 must document the explicit owner_request composition boundary in the architecture, rather than silently broadening contact-facing workers.
